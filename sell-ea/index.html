REB BOT MARKET — POLICY AND TERMS OF SELLING
Last updated: September 2026

By submitting an Expert Advisor (EA) or any trading-software product to REB Bot Market, the Seller agrees to comply with the following policies and terms. Clicking “Submit EA For Review” means that the Seller has read, understood, and accepted these Policy and Terms of Selling.

1. Seller Rights and Responsibilities

The Seller is fully responsible for every EA submitted to REB Bot Market. The Seller must ensure that the EA and all submitted information are:

  • Truthful and accurate.
  • Not misleading to Buyers.
  • Not infringing any third-party rights.
  • Free from functions intended to cause harm.
  • Free from malware, viruses, or malicious code.
  • Usable in a manner consistent with the published Description.

The Seller must provide enough information for a Buyer to understand the EA’s functions, risks, and intended use before purchase.

2. Ownership and Intellectual Property

A Seller may list only an EA that the Seller owns, developed, or is legally authorized to distribute and sell. The following are prohibited:

  • Stolen or copied EAs.
  • Cracked EAs.
  • Decompiled EAs.
  • EAs containing source code copied from another developer without authorization.
  • EAs using another party’s logo, image, source code, or intellectual property without permission.
  • EAs purchased from another marketplace and resold without resale rights.

If REB receives a copyright complaint or ownership dispute, REB may temporarily suspend the Listing while the matter is reviewed.

3. Admin Review and Approval

Every EA must pass REB Admin review before it may be published on the Marketplace. Submission does not guarantee approval. Admin may:

  • Approve a Listing.
  • Reject a Listing.
  • Request changes to the Description.
  • Request additional documents.
  • Request a new EA file.
  • Request additional Backtest or Real Account evidence.
  • Suspend a Listing.
  • Remove a Listing.
  • Require the Seller to update the EA before continued sale is permitted.

A Listing with Pending Review status will not be shown publicly until Admin approval.

4. EA File and Security Requirements

Uploaded EA files must use formats permitted by REB Bot Market. For MetaTrader 5, permitted formats may include .mq5 and .ex5. Sellers must not upload files containing:

  • Malware, viruses, spyware, or trojans.
  • Credential stealers or keyloggers.
  • Unauthorized remote access or hidden malicious code.
  • Functions that steal account information or trading passwords.
  • Functions that damage a computer, VPS, MT5 installation, or trading account.

REB may perform security and functional reviews before approval.

5. Information the Seller Must Provide

Each Listing must contain a clear Description that enables a Buyer to understand the EA before purchase. Information should include:

  • EA Name, Strategy, Supported Symbols, and Recommended Timeframe.
  • Minimum Deposit, Account Type, and Recommended Leverage.
  • Risk Settings, Lot Size Method, Stop Loss, and Take Profit.
  • Trading Session, Broker Requirements, and VPS Requirements.
  • Grid, Martingale, Hedging, or Recovery functions, if used.
  • Maximum Risk or Drawdown warnings.
  • License Information, Installation Instructions, and Customer Support Information.

The Seller must not hide functions that can materially increase risk.

6. Pricing Policy

An EA may be listed as a Free EA or Paid EA. Paid EAs may offer Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime licenses. The Seller must enter accurate pricing. The price shown to the Buyer at Checkout is the price applicable to that purchase unless an authorized REB promotion or discount applies.

The Seller must not use a Description or Message to direct a Buyer to make off-platform payments for the purpose of avoiding Marketplace fees or the REB payment system. REB may set commissions, Marketplace fees, payment-processing fees, or other commercial terms under platform policy.

7. Profit and Performance Claims

The Seller must present EA performance information clearly, accurately, and in a verifiable manner so that Users/Buyers can evaluate the EA before deciding to purchase. False or misleading claims about profit, performance, win rate, drawdown, or trading results are prohibited.

Claims that imply guaranteed profitability are prohibited, including “Guaranteed Profit”, “100% Win Rate”, “Zero Risk”, “Cannot Lose”, “Guaranteed Monthly Return”, or equivalent wording.

7.1 Mandatory 7-Day Trial Version

Every EA offered on REB Bot Market must provide a Trial Version so Buyers can test it before purchase. The Trial Version must:

  • Remain usable for at least 7 days.
  • Include enough core functionality to evaluate the EA.
  • Clearly disclose any limitations.
  • Not use different trading logic from the Paid Version to make trial results appear better than the real product.
  • Not disable essential functions to the extent that meaningful evaluation becomes impossible.

The Trial may be limited by time, account, license, or another safe method, but it must remain available for at least 7 days. An EA without a Trial Version may be refused approval.

7.2 Performance Disclosure

The Seller must disclose material performance information such as Profit/Loss, Drawdown, Total Trades, Win Rate, Trading Period, Initial Deposit, Balance, Equity, Symbol, Timeframe, Lot Size, Risk Setting, Broker, and Account Type. Losses, floating losses, drawdown, or other material risk factors must not be hidden to make performance appear better than it actually is.

7.3 Minimum One-Month Backtest Result

Every EA must provide a Backtest Result covering at least one month before the Listing can be approved. At minimum, the Backtest should show Testing Period, Symbol, Timeframe, Initial Deposit, Net Profit, Maximum Drawdown, Total Trades, Win/Loss Statistics, and Lot Size or Risk Setting. Longer tests are encouraged. A Backtest must be identified as a Historical Test / Simulation and must not be represented as a Real Trading Result.

7.4 Real Account Result

In addition to a Backtest, the Seller must provide a Real Account Trading Result showing that the EA has been used in a real trading environment. The Real Account Result should include Trading Period, Trading History, Balance, Equity, Profit/Loss, Drawdown, Open Positions, Closed Positions, Symbol, Account Type, and Broker. A Demo Account must not be represented as a Real Account.

7.5 Investor Account / Read-Only Account

The EA Description must provide an Investor Account / Read-Only Account that allows Buyers to monitor the Real Account’s performance. The information should include Broker Name, Trading Server, Account Number/Login, and Investor Password/Read-Only Password. Access must be read-only.

The Seller must never provide a Master Trading Password or any password that permits another person to trade, withdraw, deposit, or control the account. The Investor Account should remain active and accessible while the Listing is active. If it becomes unavailable, the Seller must update the credentials, and REB may suspend the Listing until corrected.

7.6 Accuracy of Trading Results

All performance evidence must be genuine. Prohibited conduct includes fake backtests, fake real accounts, fake investor accounts, fake profit screenshots, edited trading history, manipulated account statements, fake withdrawal/deposit evidence, hiding losses or drawdown, presenting Demo results as Real results, using another EA’s results, or editing screenshots to create a false impression.

If falsification is found, the Listing may be Rejected, Suspended, or Removed, and the Seller Account may be restricted.

7.7 Past Performance

Backtests, historical results, and real-account results are provided for evaluation only. Past Performance does not guarantee future results. Results can vary because of broker conditions, spread, slippage, latency, liquidity, leverage, VPS, execution speed, account type, risk settings, and market conditions.

8. Cover Image, Gallery, Video, and Marketing Content

Media must directly relate to the EA being listed. Cover images, screenshots, galleries, and videos must not contain fake profits, fake trading statements, fake reviews, fake customer testimonials, misleading imagery, or third-party content used without permission. If a YouTube video is provided, it should relate to the EA’s functions, installation, strategy, live trading, or performance information.

9. Trading Risk Disclosure

Trading involves risk and may result in loss of capital. Approval and publication on REB Bot Market do not mean that REB guarantees profit, win rate, future performance, capital protection, maximum drawdown, or EA reliability in every market condition. The Buyer is responsible for risk settings, lot size, capital allocation, and the decision to use an EA.

10. REB Does Not Provide Investment Advice

REB Bot Market is a marketplace for trading software. EA listings, trading results, backtests, strategies, and Seller descriptions are not personalized financial or investment advice from REB. Buyers should evaluate products and make their own decisions.

11. EA Updates and Compatibility

The Seller is responsible for maintaining the EA. If the EA stops working because of an MT5 update, broker change, API change, license issue, server change, or operating-system update, the Seller should provide an update within a reasonable period when a fix is feasible. A major update that materially changes trading logic may require another Admin review.

12. Customer Support

The Seller must provide the support advertised in the Listing and should clearly state Support Method, Support Time, Installation Support, License Activation, Update Policy, Supported Broker, and Supported Account Type. A Seller must not advertise 24/7 support unless it can actually be provided.

13. License Policy

The Seller must clearly disclose license terms, including the number of accounts allowed, number of devices/VPS, license duration, reset policy, Hardware ID limitations, broker limitations, and Demo/Real Account limitations. After purchase, the Seller must not materially change a Buyer’s license terms without a reasonable basis or in a way that conflicts with the terms advertised at the time of purchase.

14. Refunds and Payment Disputes

Refunds, cancellations, payment disputes, and license replacements are governed by the REB Marketplace Refund Policy in effect when the Buyer purchases. The Seller must not promise a refund policy that conflicts with REB Marketplace Policy. If a Seller provides a product that materially differs from the Description, the file does not work, or there is evidence of fraud, REB may review the matter.

15. Prohibited Selling Activities

Sellers must not:

  • Create fake accounts to review their own products, fake purchases, fake ratings, or fake testimonials.
  • Spam Buyers or pressure Buyers to pay outside the Marketplace.
  • Sell malware or stolen EAs.
  • Bypass the REB License System or REB Payment System.
  • Use the platform to steal personal data.
  • Use an Investor Account as a pretext to request a Buyer’s Master Password.
  • Impersonate another developer or company.

16. Suspension or Removal of an EA Listing

REB may Suspend, Hide, Reject, or Remove a Listing if the EA violates policy, creates a security risk, the Seller provides false information, the Trial Version does not work, the Investor Account cannot be verified, Real Account evidence is invalid, a copyright complaint exists, the EA does not operate as described, promised support is not provided, fraud or abuse occurs, or the Seller attempts to bypass payment or licensing systems.

Repeated policy violations may result in restrictions on Seller features. Serious cases such as fraud, malware, account theft, copyright abuse, payment fraud, or security attacks may result in Seller Account suspension or termination depending on the circumstances.

REB Bot Market provides a platform where Sellers and Buyers can list, test, and purchase trading software. Admin approval is intended to review Listing Requirements and Marketplace Policy only; it is not a guarantee of profit or a statement that an EA is suitable for every Buyer. Performance may differ because of broker conditions, spread, slippage, liquidity, VPS, execution speed, market conditions, and risk settings.

EA Bot 销售与上架政策

REB BOT MARKET — 销售政策与条款
最后更新：2026 年 9 月

向 REB Bot Market 提交 Expert Advisor (EA) 或任何交易软件产品，即表示卖家（Seller）同意遵守以下政策与条款。点击 “Submit EA For Review” 即表示卖家已经阅读、理解并接受本销售政策与条款。

1. 卖家的权利与责任

卖家对提交至 REB Bot Market 的 EA 承担全部责任。卖家必须确保 EA 及提交的信息：

  • 真实、准确。
  • 不会误导买家。
  • 不侵犯任何第三方权利。
  • 不包含蓄意造成损害的功能。
  • 不包含恶意软件、病毒或恶意代码。
  • 能够按照所公布的 Description 正常使用。

卖家必须提供足够的信息，使买家在购买前能够了解 EA 的功能、风险以及正确使用方式。

2. 所有权与知识产权

卖家只能上架自己拥有、自己开发，或依法获得分发和销售授权的 EA。禁止：

  • 盗取或抄袭的 EA。
  • Cracked EA。
  • Decompiled EA。
  • 未经授权复制其他开发者源代码的 EA。
  • 未经许可使用他人的 Logo、Image、Source Code 或其他知识产权。
  • 从其他 Marketplace 购买后，在没有转售权的情况下再次销售。

如出现版权投诉或所有权争议，REB 可在调查期间临时暂停该 Listing。

3. Admin 审核与批准

所有 EA 必须通过 REB Admin 审核后才能发布到 Marketplace。提交并不代表自动批准。Admin 有权：

  • Approve Listing。
  • Reject Listing。
  • 要求修改 Description。
  • 要求补充文件。
  • 要求重新提供 EA File。
  • 要求补充 Backtest 或 Real Account Evidence。
  • 暂停 Listing。
  • Remove Listing。
  • 要求卖家更新 EA 后才允许继续销售。

Status 为 Pending Review 的 Listing 在 Admin 批准前不会公开显示。

4. EA File 与安全要求

卖家上传的 EA File 必须使用 REB Bot Market 允许的格式。MetaTrader 5 可包括 .mq5 和 .ex5。不得上传包含以下内容的文件：

  • Malware、Virus、Spyware 或 Trojan。
  • Credential Stealer 或 Keylogger。
  • Unauthorized Remote Access 或隐藏的恶意代码。
  • 窃取账户信息或 Trading Password 的功能。
  • 损害 Computer、VPS、MT5 或 Trading Account 的功能。

REB 可在批准前进行 Security Review 与 Functional Review。

5. 卖家必须提供的信息

EA Listing 必须包含清楚的 Description，使买家能够在购买前理解产品。信息应包括：

  • EA Name、Strategy、Supported Symbols、Recommended Timeframe。
  • Minimum Deposit、Account Type、Recommended Leverage。
  • Risk Settings、Lot Size Method、Stop Loss、Take Profit。
  • Trading Session、Broker Requirement、VPS Requirement。
  • 如使用 Grid、Martingale、Hedging 或 Recovery Function，必须说明。
  • Maximum Risk 或 Drawdown Warning。
  • License Information、Installation Instruction、Customer Support Information。

卖家不得向买家隐瞒会显著提高风险的重要功能。

6. 价格与 Pricing Policy

EA 可上架为 Free EA 或 Paid EA。Paid EA 可设置 Monthly、Annual 和 Lifetime License。卖家必须填写准确价格。除非 REB 授权的 Promotion 或 Discount 适用，否则买家在 Checkout 时看到的价格即为该次购买适用的价格。

卖家不得通过 Description 或 Message 引导买家到 REB 系统之外付款，以逃避 Marketplace Fee 或 Payment System。REB 可根据平台政策设置 Commission、Marketplace Fee、Payment Processing Fee 或其他商业条款。

7. Profit 与 Performance 声明

卖家必须以清楚、准确且可核实的方式展示 EA 的表现信息，使 User/Buyer 能够在购买前进行评估。严禁对 Profit、Performance、Win Rate、Drawdown 或 Trading Result 作虚假或误导性陈述。

不得使用暗示收益有保证的说法，例如 “Guaranteed Profit”、“100% Win Rate”、“Zero Risk”、“Cannot Lose”、“Guaranteed Monthly Return” 或具有相同含义的表达。

7.1 强制 7 天 Trial Version

在 REB Bot Market 上架的 EA 必须提供 Trial Version，让买家在购买前测试。Trial Version 必须：

  • 至少可使用 7 天。
  • 包含足够的核心功能，以便评估 EA。
  • 如有限制，必须清楚说明。
  • 不得通过与 Paid Version 不同的 Trading Logic 故意让 Trial Result 看起来更好。
  • 不得禁用关键功能到导致买家无法有效测试 EA 的程度。

Trial 可通过 Time、Account、License 或其他安全方式限制，但使用期限不得少于 7 天。没有 Trial Version 的 EA 可能不会被批准。

7.2 Performance 信息披露

卖家必须披露重要 Performance 信息，包括 Profit/Loss、Drawdown、Total Trades、Win Rate、Trading Period、Initial Deposit、Balance、Equity、Symbol、Timeframe、Lot Size、Risk Setting、Broker 和 Account Type。不得隐藏 Loss、Floating Loss、Drawdown 或其他重要 Risk Factor，以使 EA 表现看起来优于实际情况。

7.3 至少 1 个月的 Backtest Result

所有 EA 在 Listing 获批前必须提供 至少 1 个月的 Backtest Result。至少应展示 Testing Period、Symbol、Timeframe、Initial Deposit、Net Profit、Maximum Drawdown、Total Trades、Win/Loss Statistics、Lot Size 或 Risk Setting。鼓励提供更长周期的 Backtest。Backtest 必须明确标注为 Historical Test / Simulation，不得冒充 Real Trading Result。

7.4 Real Account Result

除 Backtest 外，卖家必须提供 Real Account Trading Result，证明 EA 在真实交易环境中使用过。建议展示 Trading Period、Trading History、Balance、Equity、Profit/Loss、Drawdown、Open Positions、Closed Positions、Symbol、Account Type 和 Broker。不得把 Demo Account 声称为 Real Account。

7.5 Investor Account / Read-Only Account

EA Description 中必须提供 Investor Account / Read-Only Account，让买家能够跟踪 Real Account 的 Performance。应提供 Broker Name、Trading Server、Account Number/Login、Investor Password/Read-Only Password。该账户必须仅具有只读权限。

卖家 不得提供 Master Trading Password，也不得提供任何能让他人 Trade、Withdraw、Deposit 或控制账户的密码。Investor Account 在 Listing Active 期间应保持可登录。如无法登录，卖家必须及时更新信息，REB 可暂停 Listing 直至问题修正。

7.6 Trading Results 的真实性

所有 Performance Evidence 必须真实。禁止 Fake Backtest、Fake Real Account、Fake Investor Account、Fake Profit Screenshot、Edited Trading History、Manipulated Account Statement、Fake Withdrawal/Deposit Evidence、隐藏 Loss 或 Drawdown、把 Demo Result 伪装成 Real Result、使用其他 EA 的结果，以及修改截图制造虚假印象。

如发现造假，Listing 可被 Rejected、Suspended 或 Removed，Seller Account 也可能受到限制。

7.7 Past Performance

Backtest、Historical Results 与 Real Account Results 仅用于评估。Past Performance does not guarantee future results. 实际结果可能因 Broker、Spread、Slippage、Latency、Liquidity、Leverage、VPS、Execution Speed、Account Type、Risk Setting 和 Market Conditions 而不同。

8. Cover Image、Gallery、Video 与 Marketing Content

卖家使用的 Media 必须与正在上架的 EA 直接相关。Cover Image、Screenshot、Photo Gallery 和 Video 不得包含 Fake Profit、Fake Trading Statement、Fake Review、Fake Customer Testimonial、误导性图片，或未经许可的第三方 Content。如提供 YouTube Video，应与 EA Function、Installation、Strategy、Live Trading 或 Performance Information 相关。

9. Trading Risk Disclosure

交易存在风险，并可能导致本金损失。EA 被批准并显示在 REB Bot Market，并不代表 REB 保证 Profit、Win Rate、Future Performance、Capital Protection、Maximum Drawdown，或 EA 在所有 Market Condition 下都可靠。买家自行负责 Risk、Lot Size、Capital 以及是否使用 EA 的决定。

REB Bot Market 是 Trading Software Marketplace。EA、Trading Result、Backtest、Strategy 或 Seller Description 的展示，不构成 REB 提供的 Personalized Financial Advice 或 Investment Advice。买家应自行评估并作出决定。

12. Customer Support

卖家必须提供 Listing 中承诺的 Support，并应明确 Support Method、Support Time、Installation Support、License Activation、Update Policy、Supported Broker、Supported Account Type。若无法真正提供，不得宣传 24/7 Support。

卖家必须清楚说明 License 条款，包括允许的 Account 数量、Device/VPS 数量、License Duration、Reset Policy、Hardware ID Limitation、Broker Limitation、Demo/Real Account Limitation。购买后，卖家不得无合理理由改变买家的 License 条款，也不得与购买时公布的条件相冲突。

14. Refund 与 Payment Dispute

Refund、Cancellation、Payment Dispute 与 License Replacement 按买家购买时有效的 REB Marketplace Refund Policy 执行。卖家不得承诺与 REB Marketplace Policy 冲突的 Refund Policy。如卖家提供的 Product 与 Description 明显不符、File 无法使用，或存在 Fraud 证据，REB 可进行调查。
